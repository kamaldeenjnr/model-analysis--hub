    Using these columns

    Where is it heading?

    QuantAI fits an exponential smoothing model and projects it forward, picking up trends and repeating seasonal patterns.

    How it works

    Real statistics, explained honestly.

    QuantAI uses standard, well-tested methods, and it tells you when a result could just be chance.

    Profiling

    Detects numbers, dates and categories, counts gaps and duplicates, and flags skewed columns and unusual values using the interquartile range.

    Relationships & regression

    Pearson correlations with significance tests, and ordinary least squares regression with standard errors, p-values, R² and an F-test. Categories are handled automatically.

    Forecasting

    Holt-Winters exponential smoothing when there's a seasonal pattern, Holt's linear trend when there isn't, with parameters chosen by best fit and an 80% range.

    A correlation or a significant coefficient shows that two things move together, not that one causes the other. For decisions with real consequences, get in touch about a full analysis.
