# QuantAI local knowledge: public-health research context

This document provides general context for research planning in Ghana and other settings. It does not contain current official statistics; users must verify current figures from the relevant primary source.

## Ghana and public-health data
Potential sources include the Ghana Statistical Service, Ghana Health Service, Ministry of Health, Demographic and Health Surveys, Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys, WHO, UNICEF and peer-reviewed studies. Check the specific survey year, target population, sampling frame, questionnaire, weighting, strata, clusters, variable definitions and data-use terms before analysis. A national estimate may not represent a region, district or small community precisely.

## Complex survey analysis
For survey data with weights, strata and primary sampling units, use software and methods that account for the survey design. Weighted point estimates and design-based standard errors may differ from unweighted analysis. Document the survey design and the source of each weight. Do not apply one survey round's weights blindly to a different round or combine rounds without understanding their design.

## Epidemiologic measures
Incidence proportion estimates the proportion of initially at-risk people who develop an outcome over a defined period. Incidence rate uses person-time in the denominator. Prevalence is the proportion with a condition at a point or period. Mortality measures require a clearly defined numerator, population and time period; maternal mortality ratio uses maternal deaths per 100,000 live births, while infant mortality rate commonly uses deaths before age one per 1,000 live births. Always verify the operational definition used by the source.

## Ethics and confidentiality
Use only data for which access and intended use are authorised. Remove direct identifiers, minimise indirect identifiers, avoid sharing individual-level health records with hosted services without a lawful basis and governance, and follow institutional ethics approval and data-sharing conditions. Public availability does not always mean unrestricted reuse. For human-participant research, consult the relevant ethics committee and applicable Ghanaian requirements.
