# Knowledge-source register

The accompanying local Markdown notes are educational summaries written for this project. They are not copied datasets and do not claim formal expert review. Before a public release, have a qualified reviewer check their content and record the review date.

Useful primary references to verify and extend the knowledge base:
- STROBE initiative: https://www.strobe-statement.org/
- CONSORT: https://www.consort-statement.org/
- PRISMA: https://www.prisma-statement.org/
- TRIPOD statement: https://www.tripod-statement.org/
- WHO data and statistics: https://www.who.int/data
- Ghana Statistical Service: https://statsghana.gov.gh/
- Ghana Health Service: https://ghs.gov.gh/
- Hugging Face PEFT documentation: https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/peft

Do not crawl or ingest entire websites blindly. Before importing external content, verify the source licence, terms of use, attribution obligations and whether redistribution or model training is permitted. Maintain source URL, version/date, licence, retrieval date and reviewer for every imported document.
